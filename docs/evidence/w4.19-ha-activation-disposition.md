> batch authorities, serialized credential identity mutation, restored bounded batch domains, and
> replaced the O(n) Redis quota transition with Quota State v2. Historical W4.19 evidence remains

## Decision

Wave 4 local engineering and algorithmic blocker closure are complete, but Checkpoint W4-C and
coordinated HA are **not complete or activated** without the mandatory external topology evidence.
This repository contains no accepted HA activation record, and every deployment surface continues
to enforce one worker and one application replica. The safe supported topology remains:

- `POLARIS_RUNTIME_MODE=standalone`;
- `WORKERS=1`;
- `POLARIS_REPLICA_COUNT=1` or Helm `replicaCount: 1`;
- SQLite, PostgreSQL, or MongoDB as the selected durable backend;
- the lifecycle-owned in-process coordination implementation.

This is a deliberate failed activation gate, not an incomplete or implicit HA rollout.

## Rollback posture

The HA operator is dry-run-first. Its rollback plan drains coordinated admission, preserves durable
authority and evidence, deploys one standalone process, and verifies health, readiness, audit, and
usage. It never deletes durable identity, audit, trace, usage, or migration records. Since no
coordinated topology was activated in W4.19, no data-plane rollback was required.
